## Extensions

Pick an extension below to read everything about it.

| Extension | What it does | Version | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Easy Poll** | A simple file mover. Select files, cut, browse to any folder and paste, with a confirmation step before anything moves | 1.1.0 | [Read more](readme/easypoll.md) |
| **Expo Announcement** | Announcement banners in six styles for your users, plus an email tool for all users or a chosen few | 1.0.0 | [Read more](readme/expoanc.md) |

---

## Which one do I need?

- Your users struggle to move files around their servers: choose **Easy Poll**.
- You want to warn users about maintenance, share news, or email your whole community: choose **Expo Announcement**.
- Want both? They work side by side and share the same dark, smooth look.

---

## What they have in common

- A modern dark design with rounded cards, subtle borders and smooth animations
- Friendly on phones and tablets
- Every icon is drawn with CSS, so there are no images or emoji to load
- Simple admin pages to adjust colours and behaviour
- Made for the Blueprint framework on Pterodactyl

---

## Requirements

- A Pterodactyl panel with the Blueprint framework installed
- A modern browser on desktop or mobile

---

## Installation

Each extension installs the same way as any other Blueprint extension: add its package to your panel through the Blueprint installer, then refresh your browser with a hard reload so the new interface loads. The detail page of each extension has the specifics.
